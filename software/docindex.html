DocIndex

Drop your scanned invoices into one folder. DocIndex reads them, files each one under the right supplier, and finds any of them again in seconds.

What changes in your office

Today

Before
  • Scans pile up in one "Scan" folder or on the desktop.
  • Someone renames and sorts them by hand, when there is time.
  • Finding last March's invoice means opening file after file.
  • "How much was that invoice?" takes ten minutes.

With DocIndex

After
  • Scan or save into the Inbox folder. That is the only step.
  • Within seconds it is in Purchase Invoices, under the supplier's name.
  • Type an amount, supplier or invoice number to find it.
  • Pick a supplier and dates to list every invoice in that period.
Tested on real paperwork. In our own test with 205 real supplier invoices, about 4 in 5 were filed on their own in 7 minutes. The rest were set aside in a Check Me folder for a person to decide. None were put in the wrong folder.

Find anything

Main features

Reads and sorts

  • Reads emailed PDFs, scans and phone photos
  • Sorts invoices, statements, credit notes, delivery orders, quotations and more
  • One folder per supplier, made automatically
  • Unclear documents wait in Check Me, never guessed

Finds

  • Search by amount, supplier, invoice number or any word
  • This month, last month, this year in one click
  • Preview pane: flip through documents with the arrow keys
  • Activity log of every file and the reason

Safe by design

  • Runs on your own PC, no cloud, no internet needed
  • Never deletes or changes a file, only moves it
  • Plain Windows folders you can open and back up as usual
  • Windows 10 or 11, any scanner that saves PDF or JPG
