Service Ticket System

Your workshop's job board. The front desk types the order once, technicians see it straight away on a tablet, and the front desk hears a chime when the car is ready.

What changes in your workshop

Today, on paper

Before
  • The order is written and walked to the workshop.
  • "Is the car ready?" means walking down or calling.
  • Photos of damage sit on different phones.
  • Hard to see later who did the job.

With the system

After
  • The order is on the workshop tablet within a second.
  • Technicians tap Start and Done with their own PIN.
  • The front desk hears a chime, with the customer's name and phone ready.
  • Photos, 360° photos and voice notes stay with the job.

The screens

Main features

Daily work

  • Service orders with plate, model, mileage, customer and complaint
  • Live job board: New, In progress, Waiting for parts, Done today
  • Done chime at the front desk
  • Search by plate or customer name

Photos and history

  • Intake photos of existing scratches
  • 360° photos: drag to look all around the car
  • Voice notes instead of typing
  • History of who created, started and finished each job

Staff and admin

  • No logins: each person confirms with their own PIN
  • Roles for advisors, job controller and technicians
  • Your shop name and logo on every screen
  • Disable staff who leave; their history stays
Runs in your shop, not in the cloud. One Windows PC runs the system; every other device just opens a web page. No internet needed for daily work. Staff outside the shop can connect privately with the free ZeroTier app. Your accounting or ERP software stays your official record for billing and parts.
