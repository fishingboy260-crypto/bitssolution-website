About us

Built by people who actually deploy this stuff.

Bits Solution Tech Sdn Bhd is a Miri-based system integrator and IT solutions provider, serving local businesses since 2020. We work across the full stack a growing business needs — point-of-sale and F&B software, enterprise networking, security, commercial computing, and custom-built workstations — sourcing, configuring and supporting each system ourselves rather than just reselling boxes.

We're an authorised partner for HPE and Dell, and carry Microsoft licensing alongside networking brands like Ruijie, Aruba and TP-Link. Whether you need one receipt printer or a full office refresh, the same team handles it end to end.
