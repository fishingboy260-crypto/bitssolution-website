POS & F&B Systems

Point-of-sale built for how your outlet actually runs.

Whether you want everything on the cloud with real-time reporting from anywhere, or a rock-solid offline system that never depends on your internet connection — we supply, install and support both. Pick the deployment that fits your outlet, not the other way around.

FeedMe

Cloud

AI-assisted, all-in-one restaurant platform — beyond just a till. Built for outlets that want data-driven decisions and automated marketing on top of daily sales.

  • AI recommendations & upselling at order time
  • Marketing tools: SMS blasts, promotions, loyalty program
  • Automated business performance insights
  • Inventory & cost control built in
  • Accepts cards, e-wallets & Apple Pay

Qsolve

Cloud

An integrated F&B management ecosystem — POS, warehouse, CRM and staff time management working together, well suited to outlets planning to grow into multiple branches.

  • POS integrated with warehouse/inventory management
  • Built-in customer relationship management (CRM)
  • Staff time & attendance management
  • Centralised control across multiple outlets
  • E-Invoice ready
Best for: outlets that want to check sales and stock from their phone while away, businesses planning multiple branches, or anyone who values automatic updates over managing their own server. Requires a stable internet connection to run at full capability.

IRS POS

Offline

A long-established retail & F&B POS system trusted by thousands of Malaysian businesses since 2002. Runs locally on your own hardware — your counter keeps working even if the internet goes down.

  • Full sales, inventory & stock control
  • Works fully offline — no dependency on internet uptime
  • Proven at scale across retail & F&B outlets nationwide
  • Barcode, receipt printer & cash drawer integration
  • E-Invoice ready

Pos2Go New

Offline

A lightweight, tablet/mobile-friendly POS with a local encrypted database — good fit for smaller outlets, stalls or pop-ups that want an affordable, no-fuss counter system.

  • Runs on tablet or mobile — low hardware cost to start
  • Encrypted local database, works offline
  • Role-based user access for staff
  • Weigh-and-sell support for scale-based items
  • Customer-facing secondary display support
Best for: outlets with unreliable internet, businesses that prefer their data to stay on local hardware, or single-outlet shops that don't need remote/multi-branch access. Support and updates are handled on-site by our team.
